## Full description

Millions of us ride the metro every day, packed in with strangers, and nobody talks. Metro Buddy is a safe, anonymous place for commuters in Delhi NCR.

- **Bowls:** anonymous communities for your line, your station, your office hub and your college. Rant about appraisals, cancelled plans or the seat wars, Reddit-style.
- **Chats:** message people without sharing your number. Phone numbers, handles and links are hidden automatically, and nobody can message you until you accept.
- **Discussion rooms:** time-boxed group chats like "Monday Office Rant".
- **Travel Buddy:** get matched with someone on the same route at the same time with a matching vibe. You only ever see the stretch you share. Meet inside the station, share your trip with family, and SOS is one tap away.
- **Vent Studio:** call in to a live audio show and let it out, anonymously. No recordings, ever.

Safety first: 18+ only, report, block and mute everywhere, real people review every report, and if someone seems to be struggling we show helplines like Tele-MANAS (14416), not bans.

Not a dating app. No photos, no real names, no swiping.

## Review notes

- 18+ is enforced at signup with a date-of-birth check.
- Report/block/mute: tap ⋯ on any post, comment, message or profile. Moderation queue at the web admin console; reports are reviewed by people, with a 24-hour SLA for serious harm.
- Contact for objectionable content: grievance@metrobuddy.in, and the in-app Safety Center.
- Location: the app never requests GPS. Users pick stations from a list.
