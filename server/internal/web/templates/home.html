Safety is the product, not a feature

  • 18+ only. Your real identity is never shown unless both of you choose to reveal it, step by step.
  • We store a station, never your GPS location. "Travel Now" expires on its own after 45 minutes.
  • Report, block or mute from anywhere. Real people review every report.
  • If someone seems at risk, we show helplines like Tele-MANAS (14416), not bans.
  • Your data, your call: itemised consent, export and deletion under the DPDP Act, 2023.
