Community guidelines

The short version: be the stranger you'd want to sit next to.

Vent about situations, not people

Rant about your boss, your college or your commute. Don't name, describe or tag real people in a way that lets others identify them.

Keep it anonymous

Don't share phone numbers, handles, addresses or photos of yourself or anyone else. Our filter hides most of these automatically. Trying to get around it is against the rules.

No harassment, hate or threats

No bullying, slurs, or attacks on religion, caste, gender, sexuality, region or disability. No threats of any kind.

Not a dating app

No sexual content, no unsolicited advances, no "DM me if you're single". Travel Buddy is for commuting company.

Adults only

You must be 18 or older. Accounts that appear to belong to minors are removed.

Meet safely

Travel buddies meet only inside station premises, at a platform and coach. Never share your home or office address. Use Share Trip and SOS if you ever feel unsafe.

If someone may be at risk

Report it with "Someone may be at risk". We respond with support and helplines, never punishment. In an emergency call 112. Tele-MANAS: 14416.

What happens when rules are broken

Depending on severity: content removal, a warning, a 24-hour mute, suspension or a permanent ban. Every action is taken by a person on our team, and you can appeal any decision from the notice you receive.
