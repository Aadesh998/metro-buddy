Who we are

Metro Buddy ("we") is the Data Fiduciary for personal data processed through the Metro Buddy apps, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we never do

  • We never store your GPS coordinates. Location is reduced to the nearest station on your device or our server, then discarded.
  • We never show your number, real name, photo or stations to other users, unless both of you choose to reveal at a given level.
  • We never sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

The raw-text vault

What other users see is the masked version of what you write. The original is kept encrypted for 90 days for legal requests and safety investigations only, then deleted. Access requires a senior trust & safety role and every access is logged.

Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. After you delete your account, we erase identifying data within 30 days. Posts may remain, attributed to "[deleted]". Consent records are kept for 3 years to show we honoured your choices.

Your rights

  • See and change your consents any time in Me → Privacy & your data.
  • Download a copy of your data (Me → Privacy & your data → Export).
  • Delete your account (30-day grace period during which you can restore it).
  • Correct your information, and nominate someone to exercise your rights.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

Processors

We use processors for hosting (Railway), storage (Cloudflare R2), SMS (MSG91, Twilio), push notifications (Expo), live audio (LiveKit) and AI safety checks (Anthropic). They process data only on our instructions.

Children

Metro Buddy is not for anyone under 18.
